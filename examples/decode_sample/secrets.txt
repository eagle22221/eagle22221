vex decode -- practice puzzles
==============================

Five short, made-up messages for the `vex decode` workbench. Each one has
been encoded or enciphered a different way; the hint under it names the
recipe that puts it back. Try one in the Decode view, or on the command
line, for example:

    vex decode --text "<the encoded line>" --recipe "from-base64"

Everything here is friendly practice data -- there are no real secrets,
passwords or keys to anything.


1. A hidden greeting -- Base64
   encoded: SGVsbG8gZnJvbSB0aGUgdmV4IGRlY29kZSB3b3JrYmVuY2ggLS0gaGFwcHkgZGVjb2Rpbmch
   hint:    plain Base64, nothing more.
            recipe:  from-base64

2. A changelog note, packed twice -- Base64 then gzip
   encoded: H4sIAAAAAAAA/wFHALj/dmV4IGNoYW5nZWxvZzogdGhlIGRlY29kZSB3b3JrYmVuY2ggbm93IGNoYWlucyBvcGVyYXRpb25zIGludG8gcmVjaXBlcy5F1Z2WRwAAAA==
   hint:    Base64 on the outside, a gzip stream on the inside, so it takes two
            steps to unwrap.
            recipe:  from-base64 | gunzip

3. A pat on the back -- ROT13
   encoded: Jryy qbar -- lbh ghearq rirel yrggre onpx gur evtug jnl ebhaq.
   hint:    every letter is rotated by 13 places; ROT13 is its own undo.
            recipe:  rot13

4. A tip, hidden with a repeating-key XOR -- hex
   encoded: 241c0a404c06181413144c161d1416164c1604100a094c041e115a1b4c1715051f1b180c1e125711091c502d35284c061f181f094c171912120e4c101e111514094b
   hint:    hex bytes XORed with a short key that repeats.
            key as text:  "puzzle"
            key as hex:   70757a7a6c65
            recipe:  from-hex | xor:key_text=puzzle
            (or xor:key=70757a7a6c65 -- same key, written in hex)
